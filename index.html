About Ananda Chandra College

Ananda Chandra College, established in 1942 during the Quit India Movement, has been a key institution in the educational development of Jalpaiguri, West Bengal. Affiliated with the University of North Bengal, the college has built a strong reputation for academic excellence and balanced co-curricular engagement. Accredited by NAAC with a B grade, it offers undergraduate Honours and General programmes in twenty subjects under the B.A. and B.Sc. streams, along with a postgraduate programme in Bengali. With over 5,000 students and 120 faculty members, the college continues to foster a vibrant academic environment and holistic development.

About National Digital Library of India

The National Digital Library of India (NDLI), developed by IIT Kharagpur under the Ministry of Education, is a unified digital platform that provides access to millions of learning resources such as books, journals, theses, reports, and videos. With content available in multiple languages and formats, it supports learners of all levels—from school students to researchers and teachers. By promoting inclusive, skill-based, and digital learning, NDLI helps bridge the digital divide and strengthens India’s educational ecosystem through wide resource sharing and easy access to knowledge.

About the Seminar

The rapid growth of digital technologies has transformed academic library services, reshaping how information is accessed and delivered. To meet evolving user expectations, libraries must adopt innovations such as AI, Open Access, digital literacy initiatives, and user-centred service models. This National Seminar aims to provide a platform for LIS professionals, academicians, researchers, policymakers, and technologists to discuss these transformations and the changing roles of library professionals. Focusing on emerging technologies, ethical issues, digital skills, and professional development, the seminar will explore strategies to strengthen the relevance, inclusiveness, and future-readiness of academic libraries in a digital, data-driven education environment.

Objectives of the Seminar

  • To explore the impact of digital technologies on academic library services.
  • To discuss the evolving roles and required competencies of library professionals.
  • To identify challenges and opportunities in the digital transformation of academic libraries.
  • To foster interdisciplinary dialogue and collaboration among LIS professionals and social science scholars.
  • To envision strategic frameworks for future-ready, resilient, and inclusive academic library systems.

Submission Guidelines
  • All papers must be submitted in Microsoft Word format.
  • Paper size: A4 (8.5" × 11"), Portrait. Margins: 1" (top & bottom); 1.5" (left & right).
  • Text: Times New Roman, 12pt, single-spaced.
  • Headings and subheadings should be bold, left-justified and numbered as: 1. First Level, 1.1 Second Level, 1.1.1 Third Level. Use double space after headings before the next paragraph.
  • The main title must not exceed 100 characters (including spaces and punctuation).
  • Manuscripts must not contain author names, affiliations, email addresses, author biographies, or any identifying information.
  • Submit two files: (i) the manuscript without author details, and (ii) a title page containing author details.
  • Provide an abstract (max 200 words) followed by six keywords.
  • Manuscript length: maximum 4000 words.
  • References must follow APA (7th edition) with proper in-text citations.
  • All illustrations (photographs, charts, graphs, drawings, diagrams) should be high-resolution, properly labeled and referenced in the text (e.g., Table 1, Figure 2, Diagram 3).
Publication Ethics

Authors must submit only original papers (with a similarity index below 10%.). Submissions containing AI-generated content will not be accepted. All papers will be screened using plagiarism detection software, and those failing to meet originality standards will be rejected.

Submission Process

Submissions should be made via email to: nationalseminar1942@gmail.com with documents in Word format. The manuscript files will be subjected to a double-blind peer review process. All accepted papers will be published in a Conference Proceedings/Edited Book with ISBN. .

Venue & Travel

Experience Jalpaiguri

Plan your visit to Ananda Chandra College and explore the culture and nature around Jalpaiguri.

How to Reach

  • By Air: Bagdogra Airport (IXB) is approximately 55 km away with regular flights from major cities.
  • By Rail: Jalpaiguri Road and New Jalpaiguri (NJP) stations offer excellent connectivity.
  • By Road: NH27 links Jalpaiguri to Siliguri, Kolkata, and Guwahati with frequent bus services.

Plan to arrive a day early to settle in and explore the Dooars region comfortably.

Gorumara National Park

  • Distance: ~26 km from Jalpaiguri
  • Highlight: Wildlife hotspot renowned for one-horned rhinoceros and lush forest grasslands.
  • Travel Time: Around 1 hour by car

Chapramari Wildlife Sanctuary

  • Distance: ~35 km from Jalpaiguri
  • Highlight: Tranquil sanctuary adjoining Gorumara, ideal for bird-watching and quiet forest walks.
  • Travel Time: Around 1 hour by car

Murti River

  • Distance: ~30 km from Jalpaiguri
  • Highlight: Serene riverside retreat perfect for picnics and unwinding amidst nature.
  • Travel Time: Around 1 hour by car

Samsing, Dooars

  • Distance: ~80 km from Jalpaiguri
  • Highlight: Misty valleys, tea gardens, and riverside trails for relaxed nature getaways.
  • Travel Time: Approximately 2–2.5 hours by car

Jalpaiguri Rajbari

  • Distance: Within Jalpaiguri city (0–5 km)
  • Highlight: Heritage palace showcasing local architecture and royal legacy.
  • Travel Time: Easily accessible within the city
